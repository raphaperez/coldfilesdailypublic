Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

ColdFilesDaily (“we”, “our”) is a personal, single-operator automation that publishes the owner’s own videos to the owner’s own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

1. Who this applies to

The tool is used only by its owner, on the owner’s own accounts. We do not collect, process or store personal data of any other users or third parties.

2. Data we access

Through the official TikTok API, and only with the owner’s explicit authorization, the tool accesses:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic) — the owner’s TikTok username, display name and avatar — used to confirm the correct account before posting.
  • Content publishing (scope video.publish) — permission to upload and publish videos to the owner’s own TikTok account.

Equivalent official APIs are used for YouTube and Instagram, limited to publishing to the owner’s own accounts.

3. How we use it

Solely to publish the owner’s own original videos to the owner’s own accounts. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling, and we never sell or share it.

4. Tokens and storage

Authorization tokens (access and refresh tokens) are stored securely as encrypted secrets in the owner’s own private infrastructure, used only to perform the publishing described above, and never shared with third parties.

5. Retention and revocation

Tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the tool. The owner can revoke the tool’s access at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and websites) and from the equivalent settings on YouTube and Instagram. Revoking access immediately stops all data access.

6. Third parties

We do not share data with any third party. Use of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram is governed by their own privacy policies.
