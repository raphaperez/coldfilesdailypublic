About

ColdFilesDaily is a short-form video channel dedicated to the world's most gripping unsolved mysteries — cold cases, strange disappearances, unexplained phenomena, forgotten history and uncracked codes. Each episode distils a real story into a tight, atmospheric 60–90 seconds, built from researched facts and narrated in a calm, documentary tone. New cases are published every day across TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.
